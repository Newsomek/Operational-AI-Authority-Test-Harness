# Concept

## Purpose

The Operational AI Authority Test Harness exists to examine whether organizational authority can become operationally consequential in an AI-enabled or automated system.

The project does not assume the proposition is correct.

It is designed to make the proposition testable and falsifiable within the limits of the harness.

## Foundational distinctions

The implementation must not collapse:

- capability and authority;
- recommendation and authority;
- technical validity and authority;
- prior approval and current authority;
- revalidation and reauthorization;
- human presence and human control;
- approval and governance;
- organizational decisions and technical enforcement;
- authority records and enforceable boundaries;
- enforceable boundaries and execution;
- expected results and actual results;
- prediction accuracy and control correctness.

## Authority-to-execution chain

The central chain is:

ORGANIZATIONAL DECISION
-> AUTHORITY RECORD
-> ENFORCEABLE BOUNDARY
-> EXECUTION EVALUATION
-> CONSEQUENCE

The execution engine must consume the current enforceable boundary.

A disposition label is not an execution rule.

## Experimental posture

The harness may produce evidence supporting the hypothesis, evidence limiting it, evidence against it, or evidence that the conceptual model is incomplete.

Any of those can be useful research results.

The project must not protect the hypothesis from contrary evidence.
